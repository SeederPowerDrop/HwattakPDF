# Editor-First App Icon Concepts

Generated as three independent bitmap concepts with OpenAI ImageGen built-in mode. The brief removes literal document pages and emphasizes direct editing actions. Pink, coral, red, and green are excluded from the final subject palette.

## D — Transform Cursor

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: premium macOS application icon concept for a PDF viewer and editor
Primary request: Transform Cursor — an icon that communicates selecting, resizing, and directly manipulating content, not merely reading documents
Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid #FF00FF chroma-key background for later background removal; one uniform color, no shadows, gradients, texture, reflections, floor plane, or lighting variation outside the icon
Subject: a large continuous-corner deep-ink navy squircle, inset generously; inside it, four oversized warm-ivory L-shaped selection corners form an open editing frame with no page underneath; a bold steel-blue arrow cursor enters from the lower right and grips a single amber-gold square resize handle, visibly pulling that corner outward
Style/medium: refined native macOS app icon, minimal soft 3D depth, precise geometric construction, subtle internal shadows only, premium independent software
Composition/framing: centered, strong three-part silhouette, 4-degree dynamic tilt, generous padding, readable at 32 px
Color palette: deep navy #18283A, warm ivory #F7F0E3, steel blue #789FC7, one amber-gold accent #D59B36
Constraints: transparent-ready crisp outer edges; no document page, no paper stack, no folded corner, no book, no reader-device imagery, no body text or document lines, no letters or PDF text, no pink/coral/red/magenta within the icon subject, no green, no price/free/donation/paywall/subscription symbolism, no coins or ribbons, no four-tile dashboard, no thin lines, no Apple logo, no Adobe logo, no watermark; do not use #FF00FF anywhere in the subject; no cast shadow on the chroma-key background
Output intent: original square 1:1 icon concept, scalable and unmistakable at small sizes
```

- Final pixel checks found no pink/magenta or green subject pixels.
- All three concepts were visually checked at 128 px and 32 px.
- These remain exploration assets; the application build icon has not been changed.
