# Freed Page app icon

- Generation mode: built-in ImageGen
- Use case: `logo-brand`
- Final asset: `Resources/AppIcon-FreedPage.png`
- Palette: ink navy `#18283B`–`#294563`, warm ivory `#FAF4E8`, coral `#EE625A`, muted blue `#789FCF`

## Final prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: production-ready 1024 x 1024 macOS app icon master
Primary request: Create an original premium macOS app icon for a free, local-first PDF viewer and editor born from frustration with subscriptions. Show liberation from a paywall, not anger.
Scene/backdrop: A single large continuous rounded-square app-icon base centered on a perfectly flat solid #00FF00 chroma-key outer background for later background removal. The #00FF00 area must surround the entire rounded-square base and be perfectly uniform with no shadow, gradient, texture, reflection, floor plane, or lighting variation. Do not use #00FF00 anywhere inside the icon.
Subject: Deep ink navy rounded-square base with generous outer padding; a centered warm-ivory paper sheet, intact and calm, with one subtle folded top-right corner; a coral-red subscription band wraps horizontally across the middle of the paper but is cleanly snapped at the center, leaving a clear gap, with the two broken ends springing slightly outward. The paper must remain completely undamaged. Add only one short muted steel-blue editorial stroke on the lower paper, if helpful.
Style/medium: restrained soft 3D macOS icon, polished native indie utility, strong geometric silhouette, subtly tactile paper, friendly and trustworthy, vector-friendly simplicity
Composition/framing: centered front view; icon base inset about 6 percent of canvas; ivory sheet occupies about 55 percent width and 68 percent height; band is bold enough to read at 32px; only three major visual masses; generous breathing room; crisp edges
Lighting/mood: quiet premium studio depth within the navy icon only, soft compact shadows, calm relief after a barrier has broken
Color palette: deep ink navy #18283B to #294563, warm ivory #FAF4E8, human coral #EE625A, optional muted blue #789FCF
Materials/textures: matte rounded base, lightly tactile paper, satin paper band; minimal texture and no noise
Constraints: no text, no letters, no PDF label, no dollar sign, no coins, no coffee cup, no lock, no chain, no flame, no angry face, no fist, no characters, no dashboard tiles, no Adobe-like ribbon symbol, no Apple logo, no brand imitation, no watermark. The broken band must read as a former subscription/paywall barrier, not a torn or damaged PDF. Preserve a clean macOS app-icon silhouette. No cast shadow outside the rounded-square base onto the #00FF00 background.
```

The generated chroma-key background was removed locally with a soft matte and despill. The final PNG was resized to 1024 × 1024 and checked at 128 px and 32 px.
